About Us

Nalmada Church (날마다교회; legal name 대한예수교장로회날마다교회, also known as Daily Church) is a registered nonprofit religious organization in the Republic of Korea, founded on July 4, 2021. "Nalmada" (날마다) is the romanization of our church name and means "daily" in Korean. That is why our official domain is nalmada.or.kr.

Our mission is to love God and serve our neighbors through worship, missions, relief, and scholarship ministries. All of the church's finances are used on a not-for-profit basis for worship, Christian education, missions, relief, and scholarships.

Ministries

Missions

Since 2021, we have supported partner missionaries serving in several countries. Through a local missionary in Myanmar, we have sent supplies needed by a local kindergarten. We also invite missionaries to lead Bible conferences at our church.

Relief

Relief is an important ministry through which we share God's love with neighbors in need, both in Korea and abroad. It includes support for surgery costs, emergency relief for refugees, and support for North Korean defectors. Overseas relief is carried out mainly through missionaries in the field.

Scholarships

Since our first scholarship in November 2021, we have awarded scholarships every February and August, before each semester begins, including scholarships for international students studying in Korea.
