About Nalmada Church (English)

Nalmada Church (날마다교회; legal name 대한예수교장로회날마다교회, also known as Daily Church) is a registered nonprofit religious organization in the Republic of Korea. "Nalmada" (날마다) is the romanization of our church name and means "daily" in Korean. That is why our official domain is nalmada.or.kr.

Our church is devoted to worship, missions, relief, and scholarship ministries, loving God and serving our neighbors. All of the church's finances are used on a not-for-profit basis for worship, education, relief, and scholarships.

교회 소개

대한예수교장로회날마다교회는 예배와 선교, 구제와 장학 사역에 힘쓰며, 하나님을 사랑하고 이웃을 섬기는 교회(비영리 종교단체)입니다. 교회의 모든 재정은 영리를 목적으로 하지 않고 예배와 교육, 구제와 장학 등 이웃을 돕는 일에 사용됩니다.
